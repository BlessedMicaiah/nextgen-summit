how the final ten

1 + 9 = 10

1 People’s Choice finalist

The Top 20 founder with the most eligible public votes advances to the live final automatically.

9 Judges’ finalists

The committee selects nine more founders on business merit and stage readiness.

If the People’s Choice winner is already inside the judges’ original nine, the next highest‑ranked founder takes the final judges’ place. Ten founders pitch either way. The finalists are announced on October 29.
