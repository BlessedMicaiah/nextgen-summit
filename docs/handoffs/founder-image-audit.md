## Source and provenance

Canva portraits come from design `DAHY3QQ86aY`, "Founders photos", exported through an
authorised export at 2160x2700 and cropped to the founder. Direct access to the embedded
media was refused (`permission_denied` on every asset id), which is why the portraits are
cropped from the page rather than taken as originals. No face was detected, recognised or
AI-generated; every crop is identified by the text printed on its own page, and confirmed
against the eleven photographs the site already had.
