Questions first?

FAQ

good questions.

When and where is Next Gen?

Friday, October 30, at the Gordon Center for Performing Arts in Maryland. Start and finish times are being finalized.

Who can attend?

Young people ages 17–29: high school students, college students and young professionals.

How much does it cost?

General admission is $20. Coming with your crew? Groups of 3 save 50%, at $10 per person. We’ve also set aside 40 scholarship tickets for young people who may not be able to cover the cost. See tickets.

Do I need to register?

Yes. Everyone needs a ticket. Get yours here.

Can I come alone?

Yes. You don't need to bring a friend or a group. Plenty of people will be meeting for the first time.

What should I wear?

To be confirmed. We'll share it before the day.

Can I volunteer?

Yes. and tell us a little about you.

Can I pitch my startup?

If you are a founder age 25 or under, yes. Twenty semifinalists are selected and ten pitch live on October 30 for a share of $10,000. See the pitch competition. Applications close October 17.

Can an organization partner with Next Gen?

Yes. and choose Partner.

Send us a message and the NextGen team will get back to you.
