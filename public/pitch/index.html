The journey

the path
to the stage.

Five steps. One stage. Apply once. A committee selects 20 semifinalists. The public helps send one founder to the live final while judges select the remaining nine.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Tell us what you’re building.

    Founder and company information, your traction so far, a short executive summary and a two‑minute pitch video.

    Applications close October 17

  2. 02

    Top 20

    Twenty founders move forward.

    A review committee reads every application, weighing innovation, growth potential, founder execution and Maryland impact.

    Semifinalists announced October 22

  3. 03

    Public vote

    Maryland gets a voice.

    The Top 20 profiles are published for public voting. The founder with the most eligible votes takes the People’s Choice place in the live final.

    Voting October 22–28

  4. 04

    Top 10

    The final lineup is revealed.

    One finalist qualifies through People’s Choice. Nine more are selected by the judging committee.

    Finalists announced October 29

  5. 05

    Live final

    Pitch on the main stage.

    The Top 10 pitch live at NextGen Summit, in front of an executive panel and the whole room.

    Live October 30

Applications open to founders age 25 and under building in Maryland.

For semifinalists

how the final ten
are chosen.

1 + 9 = 10

1 People’s Choice finalist

The Top 20 founder with the most eligible public votes advances to the live final automatically.

9 Judges’ finalists

The committee selects nine more founders on business merit and stage readiness.

If the People’s Choice winner is already inside the judges’ original nine, the next highest‑ranked founder takes the final judges’ place. Ten founders pitch either way.
