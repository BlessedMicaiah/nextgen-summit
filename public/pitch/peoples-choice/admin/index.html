Voting

Open and close voting, and set the deadline the server enforces. A deadline the browser can argue with is not a deadline, so this value would be checked again on every submitted vote.

Results and fraud review

Totals stay hidden from the public while voting is open and are published only after approval. Review flags surface clusters that look automated rather than human.

What this screen needs before it is real:
  • Staff authentication, with roles, and an audit trail of who changed what.
  • A votes table with a unique index on (competition, voter) so a second vote cannot be written at all.
  • Server-side deadline and eligibility checks on every submission.
  • Rate limiting and bot protection in front of the vote endpoint.
  • Row-level security so a voter can read their own ballot and nobody else’s.
  • Storage for portraits and a field for the pitch video link.
