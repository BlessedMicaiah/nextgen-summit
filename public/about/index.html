About NextGen

Two words we want every person in the room on October 30 to believe about themselves by the time they leave.

Why NextGen exists

Most people do not lack ambition. They lack access: to the room, to the person who has done it, to the honest answer about what comes next.

NextGen Summit is a one-day experience built to close that gap. It brings together people, ideas and opportunities so that what felt out of reach starts to look like a plan.

What we believe

Come for fresh perspective.
Leave with direction.

What guides the day

Four things shape every part of the experience. We call them ROAD.

  1. RelationshipsThe people you meet are the point, not the programming around them.
  2. OpportunityTen founders pitch live for a share of $10,000. Others find the opening they were looking for.
  3. AccessCandid conversations with people who have already walked it, in the same room as you.
  4. DirectionYou leave knowing your next move, not just feeling good about the day.

Who we are bringing together

NextGen is built for anyone facing a decision about what comes next.

  • High school students whose future is bigger than they think.
  • College students and recent graduates turning interests and skills into a next move.
  • Young founders and creators ready to move an idea forward.
  • Owners and professionals looking for fresh ideas and relationships.
  • Anyone ready for a new chapter who needs a spark and a reason to start.
